Projects

Data Cleaning EDA Machine Learning

Heart Disease Prediction

Cleaned and explored a clinical dataset of 270 patients with 13 medical features, then trained and compared three models (Logistic Regression, KNN, Random Forest) to predict heart disease. The best model reached 85% accuracy on unseen test data, with 92% recall on patients who had the disease.

Key findings: chest pain type, number of major vessels, exercise-induced angina, and thal results were among the features most related to heart disease.

Tools: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn

Data Cleaning EDA Regression

House Price Prediction

Cleaned a real-estate dataset of 1,460 houses with 79 features, including fixing "missing" values that actually meant a house had no pool, garage, or alley. Compared Ridge Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting to predict sale prices.

The best model explained about 92% of price variation (R² 0.93 on held-out data) with an average error of about $14,300 on houses with a median price of $167,000. Overall quality, total living area, and neighborhood were the strongest drivers of price.

Tools: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn
